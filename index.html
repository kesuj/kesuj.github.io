About

A little about me

Electrical and Electronics Engineer, graduated from Kathmandu University in 2026. Most of my coursework and project work was on the power side: hydropower, transmission, protection and solar.

That included a short internship at the Nupche Likhu Hydropower Project and an agrivoltaics pilot in Dhulikhel with the Nepal Technology Innovation Center. Alongside that, embedded work with Arduino and ESP32, usually simulated in Proteus or MATLAB first, some machine learning in Python, and video editing and camera work when a project needs it.

G. Nepal is a small company a few of us started in Kathmandu. It works on robotics, drones, AI and STEM education for schools and government offices. After the Bhote Koshi flood in August 2026 the team, including me, was sent to Rasuwa and Nuwakot for drone surveying and mapping.
