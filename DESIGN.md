# Design notes

Reference: Dribbble, "UI Flip – Overhaul of my personal website & portfolio" (Ivo Ivanov), turned dark.

## Layout
- One 1200px frame with 1px guide lines left and right; every section is separated by a line with small cross marks where it meets the frame.
- Sticky nav: wordmark, mono section links, white pill "Let's talk".
- Hero: mono line, two-tone headline (grey and white words), places as a text "logo row", intro and two buttons, two project screenshots.
- Work: big rows (image left, text right) for the power and solar projects, a 3-column grid of builds, then Rasuwa as the closing feature.
- About with a black-and-white photo and a handwritten signature, experience accordion, tools grid, G. Nepal ventures, GitHub repos, big "Have a project in mind?" contact, giant dim wordmark at the bottom.
